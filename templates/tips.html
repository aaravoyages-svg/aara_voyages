Pro Travel Secrets

Bonus tips from frequent flyers

🗺️

Download Offline Maps

Save Google Maps for offline use before you travel. You'll thank yourself when Wi-Fi is unavailable in remote areas.

🔌

Universal Adapter

Carry a universal power adapter with USB ports. Different countries use different plug types — be prepared for all of them.

💊

Mini First-Aid Kit

Pack band-aids, paracetamol, antacids, and any prescription medication. Pharmacies abroad may not have your usual brands.

📱

Local SIM or eSIM

Buy a local SIM card or activate an eSIM on arrival for affordable data. Avoid expensive roaming charges from your home carrier.
